## 📁 Files & Ready-to-Use Assets

1. **`index.html`** - The luxury mobile-optimized landing page featuring your cover photo, centered circular logo with gold border, verified badge, all 4 social buttons, "Share Page", and "Save Contact" vCard.
2. **`scanner.html`** - Camera QR scanner that works with your phone or PC camera, plus a "Scan from Gallery / Image" option.
3. **`qr-studio.html`** - Interactive QR studio where you can customize QR colors, toggle logos, switch between Table Stand / Card views, and download high-resolution PNGs or print directly.
4. **`maison_evora_qr_with_logo.png`** - High-resolution (735x735) QR code with the Maison Evora logo embedded in the center.
5. **`maison_evora_flyer.png`** - High-resolution (1200x1800) luxury printable poster/flyer ready for print, social media, or shop counter display.
6. **`maison_evora_qr_classic.png`** - Standard black & white high-contrast QR code.
7. **`start.bat`** - Double-click anytime to launch a local preview server on your PC and local Wi-Fi.

## 🚀 How to Run & Preview

### Method 1: Instant Local Browser View
Simply double-click **`index.html`** or **`qr-studio.html`** to open in any web browser.

### Method 2: Test on Mobile Phone via Wi-Fi
1. Double-click **`start.bat`** (or run `python -m http.server 8000`).
2. Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi.
3. On your phone, open your camera or browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://192.168.0.182:8000
   ```
4. Or scan **`maison_evora_qr_local_test.png`** with your phone camera!

## 🌐 Free 1-Minute Worldwide Hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel)

To allow anyone in the world to scan your QR code anytime:
1. **GitHub Pages (Free)**:
   - Create a free GitHub repository (e.g. `maison-evora`).
   - Upload the files in this folder.
   - Go to **Settings** > **Pages** > Select `main` branch > **Save**.
   - Your live link will be `https://<your-username>.github.io/maison-evora/`.
2. Open **`qr-studio.html`**, paste your live URL into the box, and download your updated high-resolution QR code!
